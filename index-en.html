Our Solutions

Every project is different. Our solutions can be contracted independently or as a sequence. In every case, what we deliver stays with you.

01Baseline Data Assessment

Before measuring anything, you need to know what you have. We assess the state of your data, your recording capacity, and your objectives — and tell you what to build first so you don't invest in the wrong things.

02Impact Measurement Framework

We define with you what success looks like and how to demonstrate it. A clear M&E indicator system, co-designed with your team, that connects every activity to the change you seek to generate.

We show where and how the territory changes over time. A rigorous, visual resource that makes visible what previously could only be described in words.

04Field Data Collection System

We turn your data into a clear, honest, and visually compelling report. Designed for the person who needs to decide whether to continue funding your work — and understand why it's worth it.

06Interactive Visualization

A tool that lets you explore your data, cross-reference variables, and communicate results dynamically. For meetings, events, or embedded directly on your own website.

Our Why

The lenga is a tree
both resilient and threatened.

The lenga (Nothofagus pumilio) is one of Patagonia's oldest and most iconic trees. It grows in the cold highlands of the Andes, and every autumn paints the mountainsides in shades of red and gold, sheltering a unique biodiversity.

Recent research shows that rising temperatures threaten its capacity for regeneration. By the end of this century, many high-altitude zones may become too warm for the lenga to survive without intervention.

At Lenga Land Lab, we draw inspiration from this tree — but also from the urgency of protecting fragile ecosystems. We believe that science, geography, and well-used information are powerful tools for acting in time.
